## Lessons Learned

- When a scoring spec lists point values for categories, ALL of them are scored. "10 points" means 10 points, not "it's just a requirement."
- Time-based bonuses described as "found within X minutes" are checkbox-based (judge validates), not automatic timer calculations.
- After a major rewrite, test with edge cases: timer at 0, all checkboxes unchecked, partial data.
